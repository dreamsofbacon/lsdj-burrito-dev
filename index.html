New here?

  1. Install LSDJ Burrito with your own LSDj ROM file.
  2. Connect the FM-1 with a USB data cable.
  3. Shape the sound live, back up songs, pick colours.
  4. Play notes: press HOME on the FM-1 for note mode. LSDj needs PROJECT › SYNC = KEYBD; the FM-1 sets it for you when SYNC is OFF (LSDj 9.2.0 to 9.4.2).

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This is experimental, unofficial firmware: back up your songs before installing.

Connect your FM-1

Turn the FM-1 on with LSDJ Burrito running, plug it in with a USB data cable, then press Connect and allow MIDI access.

If it doesn't connect

No FM-1 to hand? Use the virtual FM-1 runs the firmware you prepared in Install in the emulator on this page (Try it), and Perform, Songs, Colours and Settings work on it as on a real one. It changes only the virtual FM-1.

Several FM-1s? Plug them all in: Connect lists them, and the list (here and in Perform) switches between them, one at a time. All screens (Perform, Screen) shows up to four FM-1s' screens at once; click one to use it. Follow the one played switches by itself to the FM-1 you play (a knob, a page, a mute, start / stop); while the one in use is played too, or two others at once, it stays put. Installing needs just one FM-1 plugged in.

No FM-1 found? Use a data cable (some charge-only cables don't carry data), check the FM-1 is on and close other music apps that may hold its MIDI port. Installing firmware is done from Install, not here.

Presets

A preset holds everything you set on the FM-1: every effect (echo and reverb too), the settings, MIDI options, FX and light switches, and the custom colours. Autosave, screen rate, battery and preset glide stay as they are. On the FM-1: press EDIT and the EDIT page stays open (nothing to hold). A key from C4 … C5 loads that preset at once. Turn the SELECT knob to pick one: the SELECT button loads it, SAVE keeps everything in it. The keys show the presets: kept ones lit, empty ones dim, the selected one blinking. EDIT again closes it. How fast the effects slide to a loaded preset: Settings › FX and lights › Preset glide.

How the effects work

Changes go to the FM-1 straight away. Keep on FM-1 stores them, as the SAVE button does. K1-K4 are the knobs that play each control on the FM-1; turn SELECT there to change pages (it stops at the first and last page: Settings › FX and lights can make it go round). A control the FM-1 changes lights up here.

Channels shows all four channels side by side for one effects page: the page the FM-1 shows, unless you pick one. The channel under the FM-1's cursor is marked ◀. Controls shared by all channels sit once, under the four. All shows every channel page at once (FILTER, DRIVE, LFO, LO-FI), the page on the FM-1's knobs marked; this browser remembers it, and picking one page goes back to one at a time.

On the FM-1 (FM-1_984 and newer) the effects are two areas. Turning a knob or SELECT pops up the channel pages FILTER, DRIVE, LFO, LO-FI for the channel under LSDj's cursor, for a moment (Settings › POPUP TIME); LSDj keeps working meanwhile. The FX button opens the master pages MASTER, ECHO, ECHO MOD and keeps them open until you press FX again; there the channel strip shows PU1 PU2 WAV NOI and keys C4-F4 mute them. SELECT turns the pages of the area shown. (FM-1_982: FX went channel pages, master pages, closed; FM-1_977-980: hold FX for MASTER.)

Master is MASTER (All FX, compressor, freeze), the shared echo and reverb, and the channel mutes. The PU1-NOI buttons are the same mutes: lit means playing. From 0.9.97 every effect is on one page only.

The echo is one echo shared by all channels: each channel sets how much it sends to it on LO-FI K3 (Echo send, one per channel), and ECHO sets its time, feedback, mix and tone, ECHO MOD its character. The reverb works the same way (0.9.97): each channel's reverb send on LO-FI K4, the REVERB page its mix, size and tone. (Before 0.9.97 the send was FILTER K2 and the filter type LO-FI K3; FM-1_980-996 also had the echo mix and time on MASTER.)

A muted channel's effects can still be changed: its column goes grey, as its panel does on the FM-1. Every effect has a MIDI CC too: see Settings › MIDI.

MASTER K1: All FX bypasses every effect without losing settings; FILTER K1: Channel FX bypasses one channel. Mutes and freeze are not saved. Muting and freeze from this page need FM-1_977 or newer; firmware before FM-1_975 shows its older layout.

Wiring: each channel goes through drive, filter, lo-fi, then the LFO (volume or pan; to the cutoff it moves the filter, dashed). Its echo send is taken after that into the one shared echo. Then the reverb (0.9.97): each channel's reverb send ("rev") goes into it (dashed when its mix is 0), and it goes into the compressor. Lit = doing something. Grey: muted, dry (Channel FX off) or All FX off.

Own windows: ⇱ opens a panel in its own window, for a second screen or a projector. Close that window (or Bring back) to put it back here. Full screen hides everything but the controls; Esc leaves it.

Files with exactly 16 extra bytes at the end can be imported after you confirm using the first 131072 bytes. The format of those extra bytes isn't checked, and your original file is never changed.

Colours

Custom theme

Start from a preset, change any colour, then send it. The FM-1 uses it as THEME: CUSTOM.

Settings

Autosave (FM-1_981): once nothing has changed for 5 seconds, the FM-1 saves the song and the effects, at most as often as you choose, without stopping the music. SAVE still saves at once. The FM-1 has it too: GLO › SETTINGS, the last line.

MIDI

What the MIDI options do, and every effect's CC

Clock in, Transport, Sync delay: with LSDj's SYNC set to MIDI, LSDj follows the incoming clock. Note in: with SYNC = KEYBD, LSDj plays incoming notes, like the FM-1's keys in note mode. Clock out: with SYNC = LSDJ, the FM-1 leads and sends MIDI clock, Start and Stop. CC to effects: the first MIDI channel controls PU1, the next PU2, then WAV and NOI. From FM-1_979 a CC change shows on the FM-1's screen (FX and lights › Show effects moved by MIDI CC) and is kept by SAVE like a knob turn.

FX and lights

What these do

SELECT goes round: off, turning SELECT past the last effects page stops there, so turning back always reaches FILTER (Channel FX). FX knobs: Channels: SELECT picks one effect and K1-K4 set it for PU1, PU2, WAV and NOI. Black keys show the page: in the effects panel, a soft light on the channel page's key: C#4 FILTER, D#4 DRIVE, F#4 LFO, G#4 LO-FI (the master pages light none, as C4-F4 show the mutes there); otherwise C#4 D#4 F#4 G#4 A#4 C#5 for LSDj's SONG, CHAIN, PHRASE, INSTR, TABLE and GROOVE screens (LSDj 9.3.9). FX knobs send their MIDI CC: on the CC to effects channels (channel 1 if that is off). The same switches are on the FM-1: GLO › FX+LIGHTS.

Where should LSDj come from?

For updates. Plug the FM-1 in over USB (it connects by itself). Takes a few minutes.

About reading from the FM-1

Reading copies the installed LSDj back to this page; it writes nothing to the FM-1. All installed sample kits start ticked. Untick kits to make room (a newer firmware can hold fewer kits); an unticked kit, or one left out of an earlier install, only comes back from your original ROM file. It does not back up songs or settings. FM-1_973 and older can't do this: install once using your ROM file first.

Sample kits

All kits are ticked. If they don't all fit, untick some: songs that use a removed kit lose those samples. To see which kits your songs play, open your songs in Songs & kits; each kit then says which songs play it. Each kit can also be saved as a .kit, replaced, renamed, edited or cleared, and empty slots can take a .kit or a kit made from your own sounds (as LSDj Patcher does; only this page's copy of your ROM changes).

What's in this version (0.9.98-dev.22)

New in 0.9.98-dev.27 (a test build): the Game Boy sound nearer a real one. The sound emulator (MiniGB) read each channel once per sample, so the tones above what 44.1 kHz can hold folded back down as tones that aren't in the music: the fizz on high pulse notes, the WAV and the noise. Its smoothing was meant to stop that but never worked (a whole-number division that always gave 0). Each channel is now smoothed over two samples: those folded-back tones are about 15 dB quieter. The WAV volume was applied twice (50 % played at 25 %, 25 % at about 6 %): now as on a Game Boy. And each channel now has a Game Boy's output stage (its DAC from 0 up to the volume, then the output capacitor that takes off the DC), which gives notes the Game Boy's thump as they start and change. One whole install.

New in 0.9.98-dev.26 (a test build): no more restarts from the hang, we hope. In the hangs so far, the FM-1's main processor (CPU0) froze on one instruction while LSDj ran on the second one (CPU1); after 6 s the FM-1 restarted, and came back with LSDj on CPU0, where the sound isn't stable. Two changes. First, the sound (LSDj's mix, every effect, USB audio) now runs from the FM-1's RAM instead of its flash memory: every hang froze CPU0 reading code from the flash, and the sound, which grew a lot with the new effects in dev.10 and USB audio in dev.13, was the heaviest user of it. Second, a safety net: when CPU0 sits on one instruction for 30 ms, the FM-1 holds CPU1 for a moment between two frames (as it does for a save while playing) and lets it go as soon as CPU0 moves again. LSDj stays on CPU1 and the sound buffer covers the pause, so you shouldn't hear it. The Log says each time it happens, and whether it worked; if it ever doesn't, the old restart still follows after 6 s. A script error on this page now goes in the Log too. Any FM-1 on dev.25 or older gets one whole install.

New in 0.9.98-dev.25 (a test build): a DJ deck in LIVE mode starts and stops on time. In LSDj's LIVE mode, pressing START on a playing chain doesn't stop it; it queues the chain again. The FM-1 stops and starts LSDj for a deck by pressing START, so in LIVE mode a deck's Stop could leave LSDj playing, and a later Play could leave the cursor blinking, then stop the chain and start it again a bar late. Now the FM-1 switches LSDj to song mode for that one press, where START stops at once, then straight back to LIVE. Also, a deck's start is now always counted on the other deck (it was "the playing deck"): before, if the other deck was still counting down to its own start, Play quietly counted on the master instead, which could look as if the page mixed up the decks. Now it counts on the other deck while that deck plays, waits to start, or runs on its own; only when the other deck has nothing going does it count on the master. The speed test and hang record from dev.24 are still in. Any FM-1 on dev.24 or older gets one whole install.

New in 0.9.98-dev.24 (a test build), for stability: the FM-1 times itself. dev.23's first recorded hang showed the background work was not what held the FM-1 up: it had half the FM-1's time to spare, yet the main loop did not move, and even the sound work ran about 40 times slower than it should. So once a second the FM-1 now times a short task three ways: on the processor alone, reading its screen memory, and reading its flash memory. A hang's Log line gives the last 8 seconds of those times, which shows which of the three slowed down and whether it came on suddenly. The Log also gives a healthy FM-1's times every 10 minutes, to compare with. Please send any hang line, and a speed test line from the same FM-1. Any FM-1 on dev.23 or older gets one whole install.

New in 0.9.98-dev.23 (a test build), for stability: a hang's record says how busy the FM-1 was. The hangs so far all stopped the FM-1's main loop dead, as if its background work (sound, buttons, USB) left it no time to run. When one hangs now, the Log line also says how much of the FM-1's time that background work took in its last second, how full its MIDI queue was, and whether the FM-1 moved at all while stuck. Please send that line if you see one. Any FM-1 on dev.22 or older gets one whole install.

New in 0.9.98-dev.22 (a test build), for stability: an FM-1 remembers why it last restarted, even after it's switched off. When one restarts by itself (a crash, a hang, its watchdog), it now keeps a short record in its own storage once it's running again, so the Log can say what happened the next time this page finds it, even if it was turned off and on meanwhile (on stage it always is). Please send that Log line if you see one. Any FM-1 on dev.21 or older gets one whole install.

Also in dev.21, for stability: a fix for the FM-1 getting stuck. dev.20's hang recorder caught one on Colin's FM-1: its main CPU was stuck talking to the chip's power controller, which it did thousands of times a second only to keep its watchdog fed; the watchdog then restarted it. It now feeds the watchdog at most 50 times a second, so that link is almost idle, and if it does get stuck the record says so. USB comes back by itself: after a restart an FM-1 sometimes ran LSDj fine but never reconnected to the computer until its cable was replugged (about 1 restart in 8 on one of Colin's FM-1s); it now attaches again by itself within seconds. The Log now also says whether each FM-1's second CPU runs (the CPU meter's R0 / R1 when it doesn't: turn it off and on). An FM-1 that just had an install or LSDj swap no longer says "RESTARTED: WATCHDOG" for an older restart, and "CPU1 DIDN'T START" always shows. Any FM-1 on dev.20 or older gets one whole install.

Also in dev.20, for stability: the hang recorder. An FM-1 that gets stuck (its main CPU stops for about 6 seconds while the sound and the second CPU go on) used to be restarted by its watchdog with nothing saying where it had stopped. Now it writes down where, then restarts as after a crash: "RESTARTED: HANG" and where on its screen as it starts again, and the Log says it with the details when this page finds it. Please send that Log line if you see one. The Log also says why every FM-1 last started (switched on, a planned restart, a dip in its supply, its watchdog, a crash or a hang). And "CPU1 DIDN'T START: TURN OFF AND ON" shows on its screen when its second CPU failed to start after a restart (the CPU meter's R1), instead of the sound going bad with nothing said. Any FM-1 on dev.19 or older gets one whole install. The LSDj slot is the same size as dev.19.

Also in dev.19: Install asks which FM-1 when more than one is plugged in, and each FM-1 shows its number big on its screen (FM-1 1, FM-1 2, the same numbers as the list), so the one on your desk is easy to tell. Before, Install took whichever FM-1 answered first. The one you choose is used on this page first (its LSDj is the one kept in Cartridges before the install) and nothing is sent to the others. An FM-1 on older firmware can't show its number yet: its row says so, and its LSDj and firmware still tell it apart. The LSDj slot is 4 KiB smaller (about a quarter of a kit) to make room: an LSDj whose kits only just fitted may need one kit left out. Why an FM-1 restarted: if one restarts by itself (a crash, or its watchdog), it now says so on its screen as it starts again ("RESTARTED: CRASH" and where), and this page writes it in the Log when it finds that FM-1 (where it crashed, after how long). It then runs LSDj on its first CPU (the CPU meter's R0, the sound may crackle) until it's turned off and on, as before: that keeps a crash from becoming a restart loop. Please send the Log line if you see one. An FM-1 on dev.18 or older gets one whole install.

Also on this page (the same firmware): Import a .sav checks the kits first. A .sav holds no samples: each song plays whatever kit the FM-1's LSDj has at the numbers it names, so songs made with other kits played other samples. Before sending, the page now says which kits the songs play that the FM-1's LSDj doesn't have (or has with too few samples), and names one of your LSDj files that has them all, to load them together from the DJ crate. (It knows the FM-1's LSDj when it's one of your cartridges in this browser; a different kit with enough samples at the right number can't be told.)

New in 0.9.98-dev.18 (a test build): every LSDj from 9.2.0 to 9.4.2 gets every helper, as 9.3.9 does: the DJ layout reads and sets their SYNC, rows and live mode, cues and the start row work, and SEQ, tempo, note mode and MIDI sync too. Before, only 9.3.9, 9.4.0 and 9.4.2 did; the others had their effects follow the cursor at most. Each version's own memory addresses were found in its program and checked on the actual firmware in the emulator (the songs-based checks on 9.2.1 and newer). Older LSDj (9.1 and before) works as before. An FM-1 on dev.17 or older gets one whole install; one that has 9.2.x or 9.3.x (not 9.3.9) needs it installed once more from this page.

Also in dev.18: what LSDj a .sav needs. A .sav doesn't say which LSDj saved it, but each song says which song format it is, and every LSDj build was checked for the format it writes, so the page tells the oldest LSDj that opens it. A crate card says "needs LSDj 9.2.1+", a song that its LSDj can't open says what it needs, and loading onto an FM-1 whose LSDj is too old says which LSDj the songs need (for example "this FM-1 runs LSDj 9.1.4… they need LSDj 9.2.1 or newer"). Which LSDj goes with these songs? now lists first the LSDj files that can open them, says which version they need, and marks the ones too old.

New in 0.9.98-dev.17 (a test build): the DJ crossfader mixes the FM-1s' USB sound too. With USB AUDIO on FULL (the default) the USB sound used to stay at full whatever the crossfader did; now it follows the crossfader (never the MASTER knob, so a recording stays at full level with the knob turned down). With no MIDI mixer chosen, the crossfader sets each FM-1's headphone and USB sound. Put away for now: Hear it here (playing the FM-1's sound on this page) and the ArduinoBoy deck; both are to come back later.

Also in dev.17: LSDj 9.4.0 and 9.4.2 get every helper, as 9.3.9 does: the DJ layout reads and sets their SYNC, rows and live mode, cues and the start row work, and SEQ, tempo and MIDI sync too. New in 0.9.98-dev.16 (a test build): Play starts on the row you cue. New in 0.9.98-dev.13 (a test build): exact timing. The FM-1's sound chip plays 44,117.6 samples a second, not 44,100; LSDj now keeps true time on it, so its tempo and pitch are exact (before, everything ran 0.04 % fast, about 24 ms a minute). USB audio follows your computer's clock: the sound is converted to exactly your computer's 44,100 samples a second, so a song at 120 BPM records at 120 BPM on your DAW's grid and stays there. The CPU meter's USB number no longer reads too low. An FM-1 on dev.12 or older gets one whole install. Not yet tested on a real FM-1.

New in 0.9.98-dev.12 (a test build): notes in tune. The Game Boy sound rounded every pulse and wave note down a little (up to 2.5 cents flat on low notes); now they're within a hundredth of a cent of a real Game Boy. The sound clock on the CPU meter: with CPU METER on (GLO), every 4th second it shows AUDIO (how many samples a second the FM-1 really plays; the ideal is 44100.00) and USB (the same against your computer's clock). Let it run a few minutes and tell us both numbers: they're what LSDj's exact timing will be set from. Next chain in the DJ layout now works with this firmware: Play starts on the first phrase of the playing deck's next chain. An FM-1 on dev.11 or older gets one whole install. Not yet tested on a real FM-1.

New in 0.9.98-dev.11 (a test build): USB audio out. The USB cable now also carries the FM-1's sound: your computer sees a sound input called Felucca (the same name as its MIDI port), stereo, 44.1 kHz, no driver needed. Pick it as the input in your DAW to record LSDj with its effects. It's always at full level: the MASTER knob and this page's volume (the DJ crossfader too) change only the headphone jack. MIDI keeps working over the same cable, so this page, sync and the DJ layout carry on while you record. The first time it's plugged in, the computer sets the FM-1 up again. TAPE is darker: from about 4 kHz down to 600 Hz at full DRIVE. An FM-1 on dev.10 or older gets one whole install. USB audio has not been tried on a real FM-1 yet.

New in 0.9.98-dev.10 (a test build): new channel effects, after the effects of other open-source FM-1 firmwares (Optimist, Felucca, Melodee). Filter TYPE: PHASR (a phaser: it sweeps by itself around CUTOFF, RESO makes it stronger; aim the LFO at the cutoff to sweep it yourself) and VOWEL (CUTOFF moves through A E I O U: the channel talks). DIST: SFOLD (a rounder fold), RECT (DRIVE to the middle gives the octave up), RING (a ring modulator, DRIVE is its pitch) and TAPE (soft saturation, darker as DRIVE goes up, the tone drifting like a worn tape). LFO SHAPE: four gates (8ths, offbeat 8ths, trance, 3-3-2): with LFO TO = VOL and DEPTH up they chop the channel in a 16-step rhythm; a synced RATE of 1 bar gives 16th steps. Tempo-synced LFOs now line up with LSDj's phrases (they start on row 00; it may take a few rows to settle). MIDI CC 70, 79 and 82 keep their old choices, so songs that use them sound the same. This page offers the new choices only to an FM-1 that has them. An FM-1 on dev.9 or older gets one whole install of this version. Not yet tested on a real FM-1: please check the CPU meter (GLO) with heavy settings.

On the effects pages, a switch's chosen value (ON, the filter type, Sync, Mode …) sits on a box in the bars' colour, and the knob names at the bottom do too, the knob turned last brighter. An FM-1 on dev.7 needs a whole install of this version. Not yet tested on a real FM-1.

New in 0.9.98-dev.7 (a test build): songs loaded from the DJ crate arrive whole. On 0.9.98-dev.6 every crate load ended with "checksum mismatch": while the songs came in, the DJ page's MIDI Start / Stop and its SYNC setting changed LSDj's SYNC in the songs being received. The crate also no longer stops with "in use elsewhere" when a DJ setting from another day points at the FM-1's port, and it writes each step to the Log.

New in 0.9.98-dev.6 (a test build): changing LSDj is fast. Once an FM-1 runs this version, installing another LSDj (or other kits) from Install or the crate rewrites only the part of the FM-1 that holds LSDj, and only what changed, instead of the whole firmware. Your songs are saved first, and the FM-1 restarts with the new LSDj. If it is cut off on the way (a cable pulled, the power gone), the FM-1 starts on its "no LSDj yet" screen, never a broken start, and pressing Install again finishes it. The first install of this version is still a full one. Not yet tested on a real FM-1.

Also new in 0.9.98-dev.6: presets. The FM-1 keeps 8 presets of its effects setup: hold EDIT and press a white key from C4 to C5 to load one; hold EDIT and press SELECT to see them and keep the current setup in one (0.9.98-dev.8 changed this: see above). PRESET GLIDE (a feature switch) moves between them smoothly. On this page: Perform › Master, Manage…, and a presets strip on each DJ deck. Not yet tested on a real FM-1.

New in 0.9.98-dev.5 (a test build): installs that get stuck in update mode carry on. After its restart into update mode, an FM-1 was sometimes missed by the computer and waited on UPDATE MODE: WAITING FOR THE INSTALLER until its cable was replugged. Now the FM-1 joins USB again by itself until the computer answers, and if nothing answers within 10 seconds this page says to unplug the cable and plug it back in, and keeps waiting (up to 2 minutes) instead of stopping. (Update mode comes from the firmware being installed, so this already helps when installing this version.) Not yet tested on a real FM-1.

New in 0.9.98-dev.4 (a test build): Play and Stop on the FM-1's own keys in the DJ layout. While an FM-1 is on a deck, its OP4 key starts the deck (on the master's next bar) and OP5 stops it. Their lights show the deck: both dim when stopped, OP4 blinking while it waits for the bar, OP4 flashing on each beat and OP5 lit while it plays. The keys light up only while this page is connected, and go back to normal when you leave the DJ layout, take the FM-1 off its deck or close the page. Not yet tested on a real FM-1.

Also new in 0.9.98-dev.4: the FM-1 shows a song import on its screen. While songs arrive from this page it shows RECEIVING with a progress bar, then SAVING SONGS, then SONGS LOADED and the open song's name. Not yet tested on a real FM-1.

New in 0.9.98-dev.3 (a test build): numbered requests between the page and the FM-1. Each request carries a number and the FM-1 answers with it, so on a busy link (two decks, the screen, the crossfader) an answer that comes late can never be taken for the next one, and the page no longer waits up to a second for it. Older firmware still works as before. Not yet tested on a real FM-1.

New in 0.9.98-dev.2: LSDj's SYNC looked after for you. Leave LSDj's PROJECT › SYNC on KEYBD: the FM-1 switches it to MIDI while the page (or a DAW) plays it and back afterwards, so START on the FM-1 plays by itself in between. SYNC = LSDJ now follows a MIDI Start too when CLOCK OUT is off, and goes back to LSDJ after Stop. If the clock stops without a Stop (the page closed, USB pulled), the FM-1 stops LSDj after 3 seconds instead of waiting for it. The DJ layout sets each deck's FM-1 up by itself (LSDj's SYNC to KEYBD while stopped; CLOCK IN, TRANSPORT and PC CUE) and says what it changed. Not yet tested on a real FM-1.

New in 0.9.98-dev.1: a DJ layout in Perform (Effects | DJ). A deck per FM-1, each with its own tempo, Sync and nudge, channel kills, a DJ filter, and a Screen | Rows switch: the FM-1's own screen, or the song's rows (click one to cue it); each deck laid out as a CDJ (the screen or rows in the display, eight pads, a jog wheel that nudges, CUE and PLAY, SYNC and a tempo fader) in its FM-1's theme colours, and a mixer with a filter knob and meter per channel. The FM-1 now tells the page each channel's phrase and song row, SYNC, live / song mode and the page volume; the page can set the FM-1's volume (the crossfader without a MIDI mixer, not saved), and live / song mode. Mixer presets for the TX-6 and the K.O. sidekick, or MIDI learn. Older firmware still works with the page; the DJ extras need 0.9.98. Not yet tested on a real FM-1: the crossfader setting the FM-1's volume, Hold and Rows, and the ArduinoBoy deck; the mixer presets are not yet checked against the mixers.

New in 0.9.97:a master reverb. Press FX for the master pages; REVERB has MIX (0 is off), SIZE (a small room to a long hall, about 0.4 to 6 s) and TONE (dark to bright). Each channel sets how much it sends to it on LO-FI K4 (RV SND), as the echo's send is on LO-FI K3; turning a send up while the mix is 0 sets the mix to 64. MIDI: CC 92 is each channel's reverb send (next to CC 91, the echo send); CC 21, 22 and 23 are the reverb's mix, size and tone. On this page: Perform › Master (the REVERB card) and a slider per channel in the Channels panel; effects files and backups keep it.

Every effect is on one page only. MASTER: ALL FX, COMP, FREEZE. FILTER: FX ON, TYPE, CUTOFF, RESO (the filter type is back next to the cutoff). LO-FI: CRUSH, DECIMATE, echo SEND, reverb SEND. ECHO: TIME, FEEDBACK, MIX, TONE. ECHO MOD: WOBBLE, SYNC, MODE. (0.9.96 had the echo send on FILTER K2, the filter type on LO-FI K3, the echo mix and time on MASTER too, and FREEZE on ECHO MOD.)

The SELECT button picks the channel the effects act on, on any LSDj screen: PU1, PU2, WAV, NOI, then back to following LSDj's cursor. Its light is on while a channel is picked; moving the cursor on the SONG screen lets go, and the pick is not saved.

MIDI sync and note mode together. With LSDj's SYNC on KEYBD (or OFF), a MIDI Start sets it to MIDI so LSDj follows the clock, and MIDI Stop sets KEYBD again, so the FM-1's keys and MIDI notes play LSDj between runs (not while it runs to the clock: LSDj takes one or the other). SYNC = LSDJ is left alone.

Also on the FM-1: a screen while it installs (a progress bar, what it's doing, and DO NOT UNPLUG); FM-1s with the newer flash chip (UC25HQ80) work (0.9.96 said SAVE FAILED there and updates failed); going back to M-VAVE's V16 from an FM-1 that had V15 works; the firmware is smaller, so there's more room for LSDj's kits.

Or one FM-1 leads: its clock and Start / Stop go on to the others (they play one SYNC DELAY behind it). On each FM-1: LSDj PROJECT › SYNC = MIDI (or KEYBD) and GLO › MIDI › CLOCK IN = ALL or USB (TRANSPORT ON lets Play and Stop start it); the leader: SYNC = LSDJ and CLOCK OUT = USB. Works with 0.9.96 too.

Risks
Installing firmware always carries some risk. If the transfer is interrupted (the cable comes out, the computer sleeps, the power drops) or the firmware has a fault, the FM-1 may not start until a working firmware is installed again; usually it can be recovered by reconnecting it in update mode, but that isn't guaranteed. This firmware is experimental and unofficial, and isn't supported by M-VAVE. The page checks the file before sending it, but that can't prove it will run correctly on your FM-1. Back up your songs first, and install at your own risk.

Controls

Click or tap the keys and buttons; drag knobs up and down or scroll over them. With Show what each control does on, every control carries a tag saying what it does in LSDj:


Yellow on the keys: LSDj's Game Boy buttons in button mode (the arrows, SELECT, START, B, A). HOME switches the keys to note mode, where they play notes instead and OCT- / OCT+ shift the octave. Blue (C4 to F4): with FX open, these mute and unmute PU1, PU2, WAV and NOI. White names on black keys: the soft light on that key shows which LSDj screen you're on (SONG, CHAIN, PHRASE, INSTR, TABLE, GROOVE).


Buttons: PLAY = START, REC = SELECT, FX opens the master effects, GLO the settings, SAVE saves your songs, SEQ switches live / song mode, hold ARP and turn KNOB1 to cue a song row, press EDIT and turn KNOB1 to set the tempo, or tap F3 in time (0.9.98-dev.9: the EDIT page stays open until EDIT again; dev.8: hold EDIT; before that, tap EDIT). SELECT picks the channel the effects act on (0.9.97 and newer: PU1, PU2, WAV, NOI, then back to following LSDj's cursor; its light is on while a channel is picked); ENV and LFO aren't used. Knobs: PRESETS = up / down, ALGORITHM = left / right, SELECT turns the effects pages, KNOB1-4 play that page's four effects, MASTER is the volume.


With the instrument focused: arrows = directions, Z/X = B/A, Shift/Enter = Select/Start. Use a MIDI keyboard feeds it MIDI like the FM-1's MIDI socket: notes play LSDj when GLO › MIDI › NOTE IN is on, and clock and CCs work as on the FM-1. Switching tabs pauses it.


Songs: Load songs (.sav) starts the virtual FM-1 with an LSDj save of yours (a running one restarts). Save songs (.sav) downloads what the virtual FM-1 last saved: press SAVE on it first. On a phone held upright the FM-1 is laid out for the screen; turn the phone for its real layout.

Limits

Stop, restart or reload throws away virtual songs and settings, even ones saved inside the virtual FM-1: use Save songs (.sav) first to keep them.
